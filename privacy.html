Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This privacy policy explains how this personal portfolio website (abdullah.weinnovate.net) collects and uses data. The site is operated by Muhammad Abdullah as a personal portfolio to showcase professional work to potential employers and clients.

What data is collected

This site uses two third-party analytics tools:

  • Google Analytics 4 — collects anonymised page views, session duration, referrer source, device type, and country. IP addresses are anonymised by Google before storage.
  • Microsoft Clarity — records anonymised session replays and heatmaps to understand how visitors interact with the site. Clarity automatically masks sensitive fields.

No personally identifiable information (name, email, etc.) is collected unless you voluntarily submit the contact form.

Why it is collected

Analytics data is used solely to understand how visitors find and use the portfolio — for example, which sections attract the most attention or whether the resume download button is visible enough. This data is never sold or shared with third parties.

Cookies

Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity set cookies in your browser to distinguish unique visits and sessions. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.

Your rights

You can opt out of analytics tracking at any time:

  • Google Analytics: install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on
  • Microsoft Clarity: visit Microsoft's Privacy Dashboard
  • Or simply use any browser with an ad-blocker — most block analytics by default
